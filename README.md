# Power-BI-Report-Analysis-Blinkit-Sales-Performance

## 1. Business Objectives & KPIs
Goal:
Analyze Blinkit’s sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution to identify optimization opportunities.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
KPI	Definition	Importance
Total Sales	Overall revenue from all items sold	Measures business growth
Average Sales	Revenue per sale	Indicates transaction value
Number of Items	Total unique products sold	Inventory & demand insights
Average Rating	Customer satisfaction score	Product & service quality

## 2. Required Visualizations & Insights

### (A) Sales Analysis by Product Attributes
### 1. Total Sales by Fat Content
Chart: Donut Chart
Insight: Identify if high/low-fat products drive more revenue.
Optimization: Adjust inventory based on fat-content sales trends.

### 2. Total Sales by Item Type
Chart: Bar Chart

Insight: Compare performance of different product categories (e.g., dairy, snacks).

Action: Promote best-selling items or discontinue low performers.

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet (Total Sales)
Chart: Stacked Column Chart

Insight: Check if certain outlets sell more high/low-fat products.

Optimization: Customize stock per outlet based on preference.

### (B) Sales Analysis by Outlet Attributes
### 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
Chart: Line Chart

Insight: Do newer/older stores perform better?

Action: If older stores lag, consider renovations or promotions.

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size
Chart: Pie/Donut Chart

Insight: Does store size impact revenue?

Optimization: Allocate resources efficiently (e.g., staffing, stock).

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location
Chart: Funnel Map (Geospatial)

Insight: Identify high/low-performing regions.

Action: Expand in strong areas, improve marketing in weak ones.

### 7. All Metrics by Outlet Type
Chart: Matrix Card (Multi-KPI Summary)

Insight: Compare sales, ratings, and volume across store types.

Optimization: Tailor strategies per outlet type (e.g., express vs. hyper stores).

## 3. Potential Business Insights
✅ Best-Selling Products: Identify top items by revenue & volume.
✅ Outlet Performance: Compare sales per location/store type.
✅ Customer Preferences: High-fat vs. low-fat product trends.
✅ Inventory Optimization: Stock more best-sellers, reduce slow-movers.
